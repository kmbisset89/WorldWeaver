# Dynamic Battle Map Collection

This collection contains **30 core 20 × 20 battle maps** designed for a 5-foot tabletop grid, representing a playable area of **100 × 100 feet** per map. The art direction follows the supplied reference: richly detailed yet uncluttered, orthographic overhead scenes with even daylight or ambient illumination, natural materials, readable cover, distinct traversal surfaces, and prominent encounter landmarks. Every final map is gridless artwork sized for a 20 × 20 VTT grid; no characters, tokens, labels, UI, compass, or written text will appear in the artwork.

Five encounters are delivered as changing environments. Their sequential stages preserve the same camera, scale, layout, and primary landmarks so they can be swapped during play. The package therefore contains **40 final map images**: 25 standalone maps and 15 stage images across five dynamic-map sequences.

## Production constraints

Each stage in a dynamic sequence will use the same square, orthographic camera position and retain the same structural elements. Changes are limited to the stated environmental event, for example, newly fallen trunks across paths, enlarging areas of flame and smoke, deeper water, collapsed supports, or ice converted to open water. The visual style is high-detail painted fantasy terrain rather than an isometric diorama. Walkable ground, difficult terrain, cover, elevation edges, walls, doors, hazards, and landmarks must remain readable at tabletop scale. The artwork must be free of characters, creatures, tokens, text, labels, grids, UI, borders, and compass marks.

> The images are intended to be placed beneath a virtual tabletop’s own 20 × 20 grid. This preserves the clean presentation shown in the supplied reference while keeping the intended 5-foot-per-square measurement precise.

## Dynamic-stage swap notes

| Sequence | Swap logic |
| --- | --- |
| Whispering Shrine | Start on 01A; replace with 01B when the storm breaks; replace with 01C when flames take hold. |
| Riverside Ruins | Use rising stages after a dam break, tide surge, or flash flood; platforms and bridge remnants remain in fixed positions. |
| Ember Granary | Advance in response to ignition or elapsed turns; add smoke, then flames, while preserving building geometry. |
| Thawing Ice Cavern | Advance after heat magic, a warm spring release, or structural fracture; ice transitions to slush and then water. |
| Collapsing Mine | Advance after blasting or a failed support; collapse debris and water intrusion change routes while retaining shaft positions. |
